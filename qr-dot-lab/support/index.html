QR Dot Lab · Support

How can we help?

Find quick answers below or email Alternix directly. This page has no support form and does not collect information.

Email support Privacy policy

Does QR Dot Lab need Internet access?

No. The current app creates and customizes QR codes locally and does not declare Android’s Internet permission.

Where is my history stored?

History and saved designs remain in the app’s private local database. Wi‑Fi QR codes are never added to history.

Why can’t the app fill my Wi‑Fi password?

Android does not expose saved Wi‑Fi passwords to third-party applications. QR Dot Lab can try to fill the network name and security type, but the password must be entered manually.

Why does auto-fill request location?

Location permission is used only after you request coordinate auto-fill. Some Android versions also require it to reveal the connected Wi‑Fi network name. You can deny it and enter all information manually.

How are files saved?

The app opens Android’s system save interface. You choose the folder or storage provider. For sharing, Android’s share sheet gives the app you select temporary access to one generated PNG.

What should I include in a support email?

Please include your phone model, Android version, QR type, app language and theme, the steps that caused the issue, and a screenshot if appropriate. Do not send passwords, private keys, recovery phrases, or confidential QR content.
