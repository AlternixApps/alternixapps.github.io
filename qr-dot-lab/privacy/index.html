QR Dot Lab · Android

Privacy Policy

Updated: September 9, 2026

Overview

QR Dot Lab is an Alternix app for creating, designing, saving, and sharing QR codes.

The app does not require registration or an account. Alternix does not use its own server to store your QR codes and does not receive the content of the codes you create.

Data stored on your device

QR Dot Lab may store the following locally:

  • created QR codes and the data used to create them;
  • design settings and any logo used;
  • history and favorites;
  • the app language, theme, and selected age category.

Core app data is kept in the app’s private storage. Wi-Fi QR codes are not added to history, so Wi-Fi passwords are not stored there.

Location and Wi-Fi

Optional autofill features can use your current coordinates or the name and security type of the connected Wi-Fi network. The relevant permissions are requested only when you use these features. The app cannot read a Wi-Fi password; it must be entered manually.

This information is used only to fill fields on your device. It is not sent to an Alternix server or used for advertising. You can decline the permission and enter the information manually.

Logos, export, and sharing

If you add a logo, Android gives QR Dot Lab access only to the file you select. A logo used in a saved design may also be stored locally with the corresponding history entry.

When exporting, you choose the destination through Android’s system interface. When sharing, the created file is sent only to the app or service you select. Some export options and sharing a QR code with a transparent background are unlocked after an optional rewarded ad.

Alternix does not upload your QR codes, logos, or exported files to its own server.

Advertising

QR Dot Lab uses Google AdMob for banner and rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process the IP address and an approximate location derived from it, app, device, and advertising identifiers where permitted, interactions with the app and ads, diagnostic data, and advertising consent settings.

These data may be used to display and measure advertising, provide analytics, prevent fraud, and maintain security. QR Dot Lab does not intentionally send QR code content, logos, precise location used for autofill, or exported files to AdMob.

Where required by law, advertising consent choices are managed through Google User Messaging Platform.

Age

Before the first advertising request, the app asks you to choose one of two age categories: 13–17 or 18 and over. QR Dot Lab does not ask for your date of birth or exact age.

For users aged 13–17, personalized advertising and remarketing are disabled, ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences, and Google’s protections for teen users are applied. For users aged 18 and over, consent and privacy options are applied according to the region.

QR Dot Lab is not intended for children under 13.

Storage, deletion, and your choices

You can delete individual history entries or all history in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling QR Dot Lab removes its private local data. Cloud backup of app data is disabled. Exported files and source logo files remain in the locations you chose and must be deleted separately.

You can decline optional permissions and enter data manually, skip optional rewarded ads, change the selected age category, and manage available advertising privacy options. Data processed by service providers is retained and deleted under their own policies.

Google services and the website

QR Dot Lab uses Google Mobile Ads and User Messaging Platform. Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit and is handled under the Google Privacy Policy.

The QR Dot Lab website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web request data, such as an IP address and access logs, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Alternix does not use its own analytics or advertising scripts on the website; the site stores only the selected theme in your browser.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app, its service providers, or applicable law changes.

For privacy questions, email alternix.apps@gmail.com or visit the support page.
