BAR Line Lab · Support

How can we help?

Quick answers for creating, scanning, editing and exporting linear barcodes. This page has no support form and does not collect the contents of your message.

Official Alternix website

Create a barcode

Choose one of the nine standards, enter the value and tap Create. BAR Line Lab validates length, allowed characters and check digits before drawing the symbol.

Scan with the camera

Open Scan a barcode on the main screen, allow camera access and place the complete symbol inside the frame. Recognition runs locally. Bright, even light and a steady camera improve results.

Edit and verify

Use the editor for colors, background, bar height, edge spacing and the readable number. Verify checks the structure and estimates contrast from one to five; it is a design aid, not ISO/IEC laboratory certification.

Export and share

PNG, JPEG and TIFF are raster formats. SVG, PDF and EPS keep vector barcode geometry for compatible editors. Android's system picker controls where files are saved, and the share button opens the system share sheet.

What to include in a report

Send your phone model, Android version, BAR Line Lab version, barcode standard and a short description. A screenshot is helpful, but remove confidential barcode values first.
