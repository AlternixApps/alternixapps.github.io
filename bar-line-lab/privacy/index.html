BAR Line Lab · Android

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026

Overview

BAR Line Lab is an Alternix app for creating, scanning, designing, and exporting barcodes. No registration or account is required. The app has no Alternix server for storing app data, and Alternix does not receive the content of barcodes you create or scan.

Data on your device

BAR Line Lab may save the following on your device:

  • created barcodes and successfully scanned values;
  • selected barcode standards and design settings;
  • price tag layouts and text;
  • history and favourites;
  • references to selected images and custom silhouette settings;
  • the app language, theme, and selected age category.

The main app data is kept in the app’s private storage. For images you add, the app keeps access only to the file you selected.

Camera and scanning

Camera access is requested only when you open the scanner. Camera frames are processed on the device using Google ML Kit. BAR Line Lab does not record video, save camera frames, or send barcode images or values to Alternix. Google may receive limited technical information about the app and device, including an installation identifier and performance data, for ML Kit diagnostics and usage analytics under Google’s rules. Other app features remain available if you do not grant camera access.

Images, export, and sharing

When you add an image, Android gives the app access only to the file you choose. You select the export location through the Android system window. When you share a created file, it is sent only to the app or service you select.

Some export options and the use of a custom barcode silhouette are available after an optional rewarded ad. If you choose not to watch the ad, that action is not completed, while the other app features remain available. Alternix does not upload your barcodes, price tag layouts, images, or exported files to its own server.

Advertising

BAR Line Lab uses Google AdMob for banner and rewarded ads. Google and its advertising partners may process:

  • the IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • app and device identifiers; the Android advertising ID is used only for eligible adult ad requests;
  • interactions with the app and ads;
  • diagnostic information and advertising consent choices.

This information is used to deliver and measure ads, provide analytics, prevent fraud, and maintain security. Barcode content, camera frames, selected images, price tag layouts, and exported files are not sent to AdMob. Advertising consent in applicable regions is managed through Google User Messaging Platform.

Age settings

Before the first ad request, the app asks you to choose either 13–17 or 18+. It does not ask for your date of birth or exact age.

For ages 13–17, personalised ads and remarketing are disabled, ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience, and the Android advertising ID is not transmitted. For ages 18+, Google determines whether consent or privacy options are required in your region. BAR Line Lab is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13.

Storage, deletion, and your choices

You can delete history in the app. Clearing the app’s data or uninstalling BAR Line Lab removes its private local data. Cloud backup of app data is disabled. Exported files and original images remain where you saved them and must be deleted separately.

You can use the app without the camera, revoke camera access in Android settings, decline optional rewarded ads, choose where files are saved or shared, change the selected age category, and manage advertising consent where this option is available. Data handled by Google or another service is retained and deleted under that provider’s rules.

Google services and the website

The app uses Google ML Kit, Google Mobile Ads, and Google User Messaging Platform. Network data handled by these services is encrypted in transit and is subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

The BAR Line Lab website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard web-request information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Alternix does not use its own analytics or advertising scripts on this website; the browser may store only your theme preference.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app, its services, or legal requirements change. The current date will always appear at the top of this page.

For privacy questions, contact alternix.apps@gmail.com. You can also visit the BAR Line Lab support page.
