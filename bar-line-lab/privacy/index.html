BAR Line Lab · Android

Privacy Policy

BAR Line Lab creates, edits, scans, checks and exports linear barcodes. Its core features work locally on your device. This policy explains what the app handles and the choices you have.

Effective and last updated: 11 August 2026

1. Overview

BAR Line Lab does not require an account and has no Alternix cloud backend. Barcode values and design settings are processed on your Android device. Alternix does not receive the content of barcodes you create or scan.

2. Camera and barcode scanning

The app asks for camera permission only when you open the barcode scanner. Camera frames are analyzed on the device with the bundled Google ML Kit barcode recognition model. The app does not record video, take photos, upload camera frames or keep them in history. You may deny or revoke camera permission and continue using manual barcode creation.

3. Local history and preferences

Created and successfully scanned barcode values, selected standards and design settings may be stored in a private local database so you can reopen and edit them. Language and theme choices are stored in app preferences. You can delete history entries in the app. Clearing app data or uninstalling BAR Line Lab removes this local data; the app disables Android backup for its own data.

4. Files, export and sharing

When you export PNG, JPEG, SVG, PDF, EPS or TIFF, Android's system file picker lets you choose the destination. Sharing creates a temporary exported file in the app cache and opens Android's share sheet. The file is sent only to the app or service you choose; its later handling is governed by that recipient. Alternix does not upload exported files.

5. Network access, ads and analytics

The current version described by this policy does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs or an Alternix online service, and its barcode features do not need Internet access. If advertising or telemetry is added in a future release, this policy and the applicable consent flow will be updated before that release.

6. Libraries and service providers

The interface uses AndroidX and Jetpack Compose. Camera preview uses CameraX, and barcode recognition uses the bundled Google ML Kit model; those app operations are performed locally. This public policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process standard web request information such as an IP address, browser details and access time under GitHub's own privacy terms.

7. Retention, security and children

Alternix has no server-side copy of your app history or barcode values. Local data remains until you delete it, clear the app data or uninstall the app. BAR Line Lab is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. Do not place secrets or regulated personal information in a barcode unless you understand how it will be shared and used.

8. Your choices

You can use manual input without camera access, revoke camera permission in Android settings, delete local history, choose where exports are saved, and cancel sharing before selecting a recipient. For privacy questions, contact alternix.apps@gmail.com.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when BAR Line Lab, its libraries or legal requirements change. The current version and update date will remain available at this URL. Support is available on the BAR Line Lab support page.
