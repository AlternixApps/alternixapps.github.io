A little about us

Alternix is an independent project where we build apps for work, communication and creative pursuits. We want them to feel straightforward in everyday use while giving people room to do more when they need it.

We keep working on our apps after release, adding features, fixing bugs and rethinking decisions that didn’t work out. Our own experience and feedback from the people who use our apps help us decide what to change.
