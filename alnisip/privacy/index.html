Privacy policy

Effective 20 September 2026

Who we are

AlniSIP is an Android SIP calling app by Alternix. Privacy questions: alternix.apps@gmail.com.

Data on your device

AlniSIP stores the SIP profiles you enter, app contacts, call history and preferences on your device. Saved credentials are encrypted using Android-protected keys. The app does not read your system address book, SMS or cellular call log.

Calls and connections

To register and make or receive calls, the app sends account identifiers, authentication information, dialled addresses, call signaling, network addresses and call audio to your chosen PBX/provider and call participants. Configured STUN/TURN services may process network addresses and relay media. Those services apply their own privacy and retention policies. Encryption in transit depends on your SIP, media and provider settings; UDP/TCP and ordinary RTP are not encrypted.

Microphone and background operation

Microphone permission is used for calls you start or answer, and for a voice test you explicitly start. An active call may continue with the screen locked. Background registration maintains your selected SIP connection for incoming calls; incoming delivery may stop if Android closes the app or the connection is lost. Alternix does not operate a call proxy or push gateway in this release.

What we do not collect

AlniSIP has no ads, analytics SDK, automatic report upload or call recording. Alternix does not receive or sell your SIP credentials, contacts, call history or call audio. Diagnostic information stays on the device unless you choose to share it.

Retention and deletion

Delete SIP profiles and app contacts in the app, and clear call history in Settings. History retains up to 500 completed calls. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes local app data; any exports you saved or shared must be deleted separately. AlniSIP does not create an Alternix account. Removing a SIP profile does not delete your provider account or provider records; contact that provider for deletion.

Support, this website and changes

If you email support, Alternix uses your email and message to respond and retains them only as needed for support or legal obligations. Do not send passwords. This website uses no analytics, ads, external fonts or cookies. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and request metadata under GitHub’s privacy statement. We will update this policy when these practices change.
