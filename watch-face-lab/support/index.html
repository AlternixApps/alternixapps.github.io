Watch Face Lab · Android

Support

Watch Face Lab is an app by Alternix for creating and installing Wear OS watch faces.

Android 9+ · Wear OS 6+

Watch Face Push · Android 16+ (watch).

Connecting your watch

When you install a design, its images and text are sent to your watch through Google services. The phone receives device details, installed watch-face identifiers and installation status to manage the transfer. Google may use its infrastructure for this connection. Steps, pulse and other readings come from providers on the watch; we do not send them to the phone or advertisers or keep a health history. Compass sensor readings are used only for the display and are not stored or sent.

Your advertising choices

We ask for an age category, 13–17 or 18+, rather than your date of birth. For users aged 13–17, personalized ads are disabled. The app is not intended for children under 13. Where required, Google asks for your advertising consent; you can change available choices in the app settings. Declining personalization does not necessarily remove ads. When adding your own photo, you can choose a rewarded ad to unlock the import. You can decline and keep editing; if no ad is available, the import remains free.
