Watch Face Lab · Android

Support

Watch Face Lab by Alternix (com.alternix.watchfacelab) is a phone editor and Wear OS companion for creating, previewing and installing watch faces. There is no Alternix account, cloud project storage, analytics backend, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. This policy covers both apps and this website.

Android 9+ · Wear OS 6+

Watch Face Push · Android 16+ (watch).

Phone, watch and displayed information

At your request, the generated watch-face package (including selected photos and text), validation token and installation commands pass to the paired watch using Google Wearable Data Layer. Device model, OS version, app availability, installed-face identifiers and transfer status return to the phone. Google transport may use Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or its infrastructure; this is not a promise that data never leaves the two devices. Transfer cache is temporary and cleaned after processing; an interruption can leave cache until the system/app removes it. Installed faces stay on the watch until replaced or removed.

Advertising, age and choice

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) displays phone banners. Adding one custom photo requires completing one rewarded video when available; cancelling preserves the project. Sending a design to the watch offers an optional video and an equally available send-without-video button; either choice sends the design, even if the optional video is skipped. If ads cannot load, either action can continue for free. There are no ads in the watch companion or generated watch faces. Before requesting ads, we ask only the age band 13–17 or 18+, not your birth date. For teens: TEEN treatment, disabled personalization, G content rating and UMP under-age-of-consent signal. This product is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights and contact

You can delete projects, remove installed faces, revoke applicable permissions, change the age band, adjust available advertising privacy choices and stop using the apps. Depending on your location, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection or complaint to a data-protection authority. Alternix has no server copy of your projects to retrieve. For questions or requests: alternix.apps@gmail.com. This policy may change with the app or legal requirements; the date above identifies this version.
