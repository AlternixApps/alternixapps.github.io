Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-09-04

About this policy

Watch Face Lab is an app by Alternix for creating and installing Wear OS watch faces. This policy covers the phone app, watch companion and website. You do not need an account, and we do not store your projects on our servers.

Your designs and photos

Projects, selected photos, settings, age category and installation history are stored on your phone to provide the editor and restore your work. The app accesses only images you choose, not your whole gallery. Photos are processed locally and are not sent to advertisers.

Connecting your watch

When you install a design, its images and text are sent to your watch through Google services. The phone receives device details, installed watch-face identifiers and installation status to manage the transfer. Google may use its infrastructure for this connection. Steps, pulse and other readings come from providers on the watch; we do not send them to the phone or advertisers or keep a health history. Compass sensor readings are used only for the display and are not stored or sent.

Advertising

The phone app uses Google AdMob for banners and rewarded ads. There are no ads on the watch. Google and advertising partners may collect and share IP addresses, approximate location, device/account identifiers, app and ad interactions, diagnostics and consent choices for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. These identifiers include the advertising ID and App Set ID; App Set ID is used for analytics and fraud prevention, not ad personalization. We do not provide your photos or health readings to AdMob.

Your advertising choices

We ask for an age category, 13–17 or 18+, rather than your date of birth. For users aged 13–17, personalized ads are disabled. The app is not intended for children under 13. Where required, Google asks for your advertising consent; you can change available choices in the app settings. Declining personalization does not necessarily remove ads. When adding your own photo, you can choose a rewarded ad to unlock the import. You can decline and keep editing; if no ad is available, the import remains free.

Storage, deletion and security

You can delete projects in the app. Clearing its data or uninstalling removes local app data; installed faces must be removed separately on the watch. We do not keep a cloud backup of your projects. App storage is protected by Android, and Google encrypts advertising data in transit. Google and its partners retain data under their own policies and may process it in other countries. If you contact support, we use your email and message to respond and keep the correspondence only as needed for support and legal obligations.

This website

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process your IP address and technical access logs. We do not use advertising or analytics scripts on the site. Your browser remembers only your theme preference.

Questions and updates

Contact Alternix at alternix.apps@gmail.com about privacy or your data. Depending on your country, you may have rights to access, correct or delete data, object to processing, or complain to a data protection authority. We update this policy when our practices change; the date above shows the latest revision.
