Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-09-03

About Watch Face Lab

Watch Face Lab by Alternix (com.alternix.watchfacelab) is a phone editor and Wear OS companion for creating, previewing and installing watch faces. There is no Alternix account, cloud project storage, analytics backend, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. This policy covers both apps and this website.

Projects, photos and storage

Names, layers, selected images, preferences, age category and installation history are stored in private app storage on the phone. A system image picker gives access only to a selected file; no camera or whole-library permission is requested. Images are processed locally and are not intentionally provided to advertisers. Delete projects in the app, or clear app data/uninstall to remove local data. System cloud backup and device-to-device app-data transfer are disabled. Clearing data also loses the non-exportable key used to sign your generated faces: an installed face may need to be removed before it can be replaced.

Phone, watch and displayed information

At your request, the generated watch-face package (including selected photos and text), validation token and installation commands pass to the paired watch using Google Wearable Data Layer. Device model, OS version, app availability, installed-face identifiers and transfer status return to the phone. Google transport may use Bluetooth, Wi-Fi or its infrastructure; this is not a promise that data never leaves the two devices. Transfer cache is temporary and cleaned after processing; an interruption can leave cache until the system/app removes it. Installed faces stay on the watch until replaced or removed.

Health, complications and compass

Time, pulse, steps, battery and other chosen complications are supplied on the watch by Wear OS/system or user-selected providers. Watch Face Lab does not send health readings to the phone, Alternix or advertising SDKs, and does not build a health history. Providers can require their own permissions and policies. Foreground compass/orientation sensors are used for display and are not retained or uploaded. Readings are informational, not medical advice or a safety/navigation guarantee.

Advertising, age and choice

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) displays phone banners. Before adding a custom photo or sending a design, you may explicitly opt into one rewarded video for that action. You can cancel without losing your project; when ads cannot load, continuing for free is offered. There are no ads in the watch companion or generated watch faces. Before requesting ads, we ask only the age band 13–17 or 18+, not your birth date. For teens: TEEN treatment, disabled personalization, G content rating and UMP under-age-of-consent signal. This product is not directed at children under 13.

Google SDK processing

Google and advertising partners may collect/share IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device/account identifiers, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and consent signals for advertising, measurement, analytics, fraud prevention and security. Google UMP manages consent where required; requests are gated by its permission. Privacy options appear in settings when required. Withdrawing personalization consent does not necessarily remove contextual/limited ads. Provider retention and international processing follow their own policies. Google reports TLS encryption for Mobile Ads transport. We do not sell project content or intentionally attach photos or health readings to ad requests.

Permissions and retention

The phone declares Internet/network-state access; advertising SDKs can add their documented advertising permissions. Wear requests Watch Face Push and activation access plus a temporary data-sync foreground service/wake lock for installation. No continuous background health/GPS collection is implemented. Local content remains until you delete it; provider-controlled advertising data follows provider retention rules. Android app isolation and signing protect local files, but no system can guarantee absolute security. This site is on GitHub Pages: GitHub may process technical connection logs. The site has no analytics/ad scripts; localStorage only remembers theme.

Your rights and contact

You can delete projects, remove installed faces, revoke applicable permissions, change the age band, adjust available advertising privacy choices and stop using the apps. Depending on your location, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection or complaint to a data-protection authority. Alternix has no server copy of your projects to retrieve. For questions or requests: alternix.apps@gmail.com. This policy may change with the app or legal requirements; the date above identifies this version.
