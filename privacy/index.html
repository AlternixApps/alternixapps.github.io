Privacy on the Alternix website

This page explains what the Alternix company website does — and, just as importantly, what it does not do.

A static website

The Alternix website does not require an account and contains no forms, advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, external fonts, or third-party JavaScript libraries.

Theme preference

When you switch between light and dark themes, the site stores only that choice in your browser’s local storage. Alternix does not receive it.

Hosting

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard request information, such as an IP address and browser details, to deliver and secure the website under its own privacy statement.

Product policies

Each Alternix application has its own privacy policy describing its features, permissions, SDKs, and data practices.

Email

If you email us, we receive the address, message, and attachments you choose to send. We use that information only to reply, investigate an issue, and keep necessary support records.
